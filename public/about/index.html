About Us

Learn about FeedYum and our vision

🍽️ Who We Are

FeedYum is an online food marketplace that connects chefs and restaurants with customers. We provide a platform where every chef or restaurant can showcase their dishes and deliver them directly to customers, while we facilitate the discovery and ordering process.

🎯 Our Vision

We aim to build the largest food lovers community in the region, where any talented chef or restaurant can reach the widest audience through an easy and enjoyable platform.

⚙️ How It Works

FeedYum is an intermediary platform – customers browse dishes and restaurants on the app, choose what they want, and order directly from the restaurant or chef. Payment is made upon delivery (cash on delivery) directly to the restaurant. FeedYum charges a fixed 4 EGP platform fee per order from restaurants and chefs, plus a service fee paid by the customer — see the breakdown below.

💸 Customer Service Fee

A small fee added to each order, paid by the customer in cash to the chef along with the order total — FeedYum then collects it from the chef's wallet.

Order AmountService Fee
Up to 150 EGP5 EGP
151 – 400 EGP8 EGP
Above 400 EGP12 EGP
🏪 Platform Fee for Chefs & Restaurants

A flat 4 EGP fee per confirmed order, automatically deducted from the chef's in-app wallet after receiving the order payment from the customer.

🔄 How Payments Flow
1Customer pays cash to chef: order subtotal + delivery fee + service fee
2Chef keeps the full amount, then FeedYum deducts 4 EGP + service fee from their wallet
3No surprises for the chef — everything is transparent from the start

📱 Platform Features

Browse dishes through short videos (Reels) - Discover new nearby restaurants and chefs - Ratings and reviews system - Follow your favorite chefs - Direct ordering with order status tracking - Complete food menus with photos and prices.

🤝 For Restaurants & Chefs

FeedYum provides restaurants and chefs with a platform to showcase their dishes and build a customer base. Each restaurant or chef can create a dedicated profile, upload their food menu, post videos and stories, and receive orders directly from customers.

🤖 AI-Powered Discovery

FeedYum's search uses AI to understand what you mean in Arabic or English. Describe a craving and it finds matching dishes and food videos, ranked by what can be delivered to you.
