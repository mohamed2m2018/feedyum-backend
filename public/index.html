How FeedYum works

From a craving to your door in three steps

😋

For food lovers

  1. Watch short videos from home chefs and restaurants near you
  2. Tap Order on any dish you like, straight from the video
  3. Pay cash on delivery, then rate and review your meal
👨‍🍳

For chefs & restaurants

  1. Create your profile and upload your menu with photos and prices
  2. Post reels and stories that show your dishes being made
  3. Receive orders directly and pay only 4 EGP per confirmed order

AI at the heart of FeedYum

FeedYum runs on AI search that understands meaning, not just keywords. Describe a craving in Arabic or English, like "something spicy with cheese" or "كشري حار", and it finds the right dishes, reels and kitchens, ranked by what can actually be delivered to you.

An AI-native food marketplace

FeedYum is an AI-native food marketplace for the Middle East. Egypt's home cooks sell on WhatsApp but don't have time to write menus. We're building a Claude-powered AI agent that turns a chef's simple description of their food into a live storefront in 60 seconds, with Arabic and English descriptions, ingredients, allergens and AI-suggested prices. FeedYum is already live on Google Play with real chefs and orders, and our AI semantic search is in production.

Frequently asked questions

What is FeedYum?

FeedYum is a food-video marketplace in Egypt. You watch short videos from local chefs and restaurants and order the dishes you see directly in the app.

How do I pay?

Payment is cash on delivery, made directly to the chef or restaurant. A small service fee applies to each order; the full breakdown is on our About page.

What does it cost chefs and restaurants?

No subscription and no upfront cost. FeedYum charges a flat 4 EGP platform fee per confirmed order.

Where can I get the app?

FeedYum is available now on Google Play. The iOS version is coming soon.

How does FeedYum use AI?

Every dish, reel and kitchen on FeedYum is indexed by AI when it is posted, so search understands what you mean in Arabic or English instead of matching exact words. Menus are translated automatically, and results are ranked by delivery to your location.

Who is behind FeedYum?

FeedYum is a product of Innovita Tech Ltd, a technology company registered in 2023 in Cairo, Egypt. FeedYum was co-founded by Mohamed Salaheldin and Mohamed Shoman.
