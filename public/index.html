How our AI works

From a chef pressing "publish" to a hungry customer finding the dish

  1. 01

    Language detection

    Every new dish, reel and kitchen profile is analysed to detect whether it was written in Arabic or English.

  2. 02

    Automatic translation

    Dish names, descriptions and menu sections are translated automatically, so every item exists in both Arabic and English.

  3. 03

    AI embeddings

    The content is converted into a semantic vector with OpenAI text-embedding-3-large, with a multilingual E5 model as an on-device fallback.

  4. 04

    Vector index

    Embeddings are stored in a Pinecone vector database, ready for meaning-based search the moment the item is published.

  5. 05

    Hybrid AI search

    A customer's query is embedded the same way and matched by meaning, then blended with text search for exact names.

  6. 06

    Delivery-aware ranking

    Results are re-ranked by distance and delivery coverage, so the top results are food that can reach you tonight.

AI questions, answered

How does FeedYum use AI?

Every dish, reel and kitchen on FeedYum is indexed by AI when it is posted. Search understands what you mean in Arabic or English instead of matching exact words, menus are translated automatically, and results are ranked by delivery to your location.

Which AI models does FeedYum use?

Our semantic search uses OpenAI's text-embedding-3-large model with a multilingual E5 model running on our own servers as a fallback, and a Pinecone vector database. Our next AI features, the food assistant and storefront agent, are being built on Anthropic's Claude.

Does the AI understand Arabic?

Yes. Every menu exists in Arabic and English, and our multilingual AI matches the same dish whether you search in Arabic script, English or a mix of both.

Does AI decide what chefs publish?

No. AI indexes and translates what chefs publish, and future AI suggestions will always be reviewed by the chef before going live.

What is FeedYum?

FeedYum is an AI-native food-video marketplace in Egypt. You watch short videos from local chefs and restaurants and order the dishes you see directly in the app.

How do I pay?

Payment is cash on delivery, made directly to the chef or restaurant. A small service fee applies to each order; the full breakdown is on our About page.

What does it cost chefs and restaurants?

No subscription and no upfront cost. FeedYum charges a flat 4 EGP platform fee per confirmed order.

Who is behind FeedYum?

FeedYum is a product of Innovita Tech Ltd, a technology company registered in 2023 in Cairo, Egypt. FeedYum was co-founded by Mohamed Salaheldin and Mohamed Shoman.
